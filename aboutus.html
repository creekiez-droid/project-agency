Our story

We got tired of watching small businesses get priced out of proper advertising.

We started Wintan consultancy because small businesses are often given the same marketing playbook as big brands — even when it doesn’t fit. We believe every business should have an equal opportunity to compete, no matter its size.

Today, Wintan provides digital marketing advice to small business owners who want to be seen beyond social media — without hiring an in-house team or signing away control of their media inventment.
