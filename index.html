The questions we get asked most, answered simply.

Ask us directly

Can I advertise on TV, radio and out-of-home screens?

Yes! We'll run your campaign programmatically. In simple terms, that means using a technology platform to buy ad space automatically, rather than calling every publisher to negotiate prices — helping us get your ads in front of people more likely to care about what you're selling.

I have $5,000, can I advertise on TV?

Yes, we can run your ads on Connected TV (apps like 7Plus, 9Now, Stan, Network 10, Paramount+, SBS, and more).

Do I need to understand the technology myself?

No. That's what we're here for. We handle the technical side and explain everything back to you in plain language, so you always know what's happening with your media investment.

Is this only for big businesses with big budgets?

Not at all — that's exactly why we started this. We specialise in making it work for small, lean businesses, with budgets and expectations to match.

Sounds great, but how much will this cost?

We believe your hard-earned dollars should work harder for you. You pay for your media. No markups. We charge an engagement fee based on the job required, agreed upfront — no surprises.
