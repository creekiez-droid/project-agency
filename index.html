New to this?

Quick answers, no jargon

The questions we get asked most, answered simply.

How do we buy ad space?

We’ll run your campaign programmatically. In simple terms, that means using a technology platform to buy ad space automatically, rather than calling every publisher and media owner to negotiate prices. It happens in the time it takes a webpage to load, helping us get your ads in front of people who are more likely to care about what you’re selling.

How is this different from Facebook or Google ads?

Facebook and Google only show your ad on their own platforms. Programmatic advertising can put your ad almost anywhere online — news sites, apps, videos, streaming services — all managed from one place.

Do I need to understand the technology myself?

No. That's what we're here for. We handle the technical side and explain everything back to you in plain language, so you always know what's happening with your money.

Is this only for big businesses with big budgets?

Not at all — that's exactly why we started this. We specialise in making it work for small, lean businesses, with budgets and expectations to match.

Okay, but what does it actually cost?

We believe your hard-earned dollars should work harder for you. You pay for your media. No markups. We charge a straightforward engagement fee based on the work required, agreed upfront — no surprises.
