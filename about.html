About Us

We are Wintan.

Our mission is to level the playing field for small businesses.


Our story

We believe every business should have an equal opportunity to compete,
no matter its size.

We started Wintan consultancy because small businesses are often given the same marketing playbook as big brands — even when it doesn't fit.

Wintan provides digital marketing advice to small business owners who want to be seen beyond social media — without hiring an in-house team or signing away control of their media investment.

Managing Partner, Strategy

Michael's specialty is turning consumer insight and big ideas into practical strategies that makes business sense.

R

Rachel Tan

Managing Partner, Operations

Rachel brings 15+ years of experience across digital advertising, adtech and client operations, with a career spanning Blis Media, News Corp and LinkedIn.
