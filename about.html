About us

We are Wintan.

Our mission is to level the playing field for small businesses.


We started Wintan consultancy because small businesses are often given the same marketing playbook as big brands — even when it doesn’t fit. We believe every business should have an equal opportunity to compete, no matter its size.

What we believe
✓

Right for you, or not at all

We’ll only recommend what we believe will genuinely help your business. If it’s not the right fit, we’ll tell you upfront — before you spend a dollar.

💬

Plain language, always

If we can't explain something simply, we haven't understood it well enough ourselves. You should never need a glossary to read your own report.

Your marketing support, when you need it

We care about your business and are here whenever you need an independent perspective, advice, or a helping hand.
