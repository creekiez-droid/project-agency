Getting started

Where do I begin?

I'm not sure where to start with marketing. Can you help?

Absolutely. We'll look at your business, your goals and what you're doing now, then identify practical steps to help you attract more customers.

How do I know which marketing activities are right for my business?

There's no one-size-fits-all approach. We'll help you understand your options and focus on the activities most likely to reach your ideal customers and support your goals.

Do I need a marketing strategy?

A clear strategy helps you understand who you're trying to reach, what you want to achieve and where to focus your time and money. It gives your marketing direction and purpose.

Can you help if I've never done much marketing before?

Of course! You don't need to be a marketing expert. We'll explain things in plain English and help you take manageable steps at a pace that suits you.

What if I'm already doing some marketing but not seeing results?

We'll review what you're doing, identify what's working and what could be improved, and help you decide where to focus your efforts next.

Channels & media

Where should I focus my marketing?

Do I need to be on every social media platform?

Not at all. It's better to focus on the channels your customers actually use than to spread yourself too thin. We'll help you choose where your efforts will have the most impact.

Should I invest in digital channels (out-of-home, connected TV, digital audio, online video and display) or social media?

It depends on your business, your audience and your goals. We'll help you understand the differences and decide whether paid advertising is the right next step.

Is having a website still important?

For many businesses, yes. Your website can help potential customers learn about your services, build trust and take the next step. We can help you identify what your website needs to do and where improvements could make a difference.

How do I know if my marketing is working?

We'll help you identify the right things to measure, whether that's website visits, enquiries, new customers or sales. The goal is to understand what's delivering value, not just collect numbers.

Do I need to spend money on advertising to get results?

Not necessarily. There are plenty of ways to build awareness and attract customers organically. We'll help you weigh up your options and decide when paid advertising might be worthwhile.

How much will this cost?

Making marketing work for your budget

How much should I budget for marketing?

It depends on your goals, your industry and what you're trying to achieve. We'll help you establish a realistic budget and prioritise the activities that make the most sense for your business.

Do I need a big budget to get started?

Not at all. You can start small, focus on the essentials and build from there. We'll help you make the most of the resources you have available.

What am I paying for when I work with you?

You're paying for experienced guidance, a clear plan and practical support to help you make better marketing decisions. Depending on your needs, this might include strategy, coordination, supplier management or hands-on marketing delivery.

Are advertising costs included in your fees?

Our fees cover the agreed work we do for you. Advertising spend, software subscriptions and external supplier costs are generally separate. We'll clarify these upfront so you know what to expect.

How do I know if my marketing investment is worthwhile?

We'll agree on what success looks like from the outset and track progress against meaningful goals. This helps you understand where your money is going and make informed decisions about what to do next.

Working together

How can we help?

What does working with you look like?

We start by understanding your business, your goals and the challenges you're facing. From there, we agree on a practical plan and the level of support you need, keeping things clear and manageable along the way.

Can you work with my existing marketing agency or freelancers?

Absolutely. We can help coordinate your existing suppliers, keep projects moving, manage deadlines and budgets, and make sure everyone is working towards the same goals.

Do you provide advice, or can you also implement the marketing?

Both. We can help you work out what needs to happen, coordinate the people involved or manage marketing activities from strategy through to execution. The support is tailored to your needs.

How much time will I need to commit?

That depends on the level of support you need. We'll agree on how involved you'd like to be, keep communication straightforward and take care of the moving parts wherever possible.

Can I start small and get more support as my business grows?

Of course. You might start with a strategy or a few priority actions, then expand the support as your needs evolve. We'll help you focus on what matters now while keeping your longer-term goals in mind.
